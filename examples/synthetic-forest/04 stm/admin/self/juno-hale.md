# Juno Hale

Juno Hale is the owner of this forest. She runs Brindlemoor Clayworks, a two-person pottery studio in
Saltmere, and sells at the Thursday market.

## Studio

She opened the studio in a rented room behind the chandlery and moved it to the old net loft on Quay Row
when the room grew too small for a second kiln.
