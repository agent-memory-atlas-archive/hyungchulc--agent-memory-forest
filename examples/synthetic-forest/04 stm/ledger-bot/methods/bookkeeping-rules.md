# Bookkeeping rules

The rules Ledger Bot follows when it keeps the studio's books, and why.

- Every invoice is in the studio's currency, with the order's date and the customer's reference.
- An order fired in two loads is invoiced once, after the second load: two invoices for one order in
  March confused the customer, who paid the first twice.
- Nothing is sent without Juno's approval, relayed by Studio Hub.
