# Odile Brask

Odile Brask is a weaver who sells at the Saltmere Thursday market, two stalls down from Juno's. What she
and Juno said, and the stall they plan to share.

## At the market

On 2042-04-17 Odile stopped at Juno's stall, bought a small celadon bowl and said she dyes her own wool
with onion skins. They agreed to swap a bowl for a scarf "some week when it's quiet".

## Shared stall

In the studio chat on 2042-05-14 Odile asked whether Juno would share a double stall at the summer fair.
Juno said yes if the fee is split evenly; Odile said she still has to ask the organisers whether a double
stall is allowed.
