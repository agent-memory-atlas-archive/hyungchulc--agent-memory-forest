# Routing lessons

What Studio Hub learned about what to tell Juno at once and which bot gets a task.

- A kiln fault goes to Juno at once, whatever the hour (her standing instruction of 2042-01-20).
- A firing that ends sooner than usual goes to Juno before she unloads. Juno, in the studio chat on
  2042-05-13: "If a firing finishes early, tell me before I open the lid."
- Anything about money goes to Ledger Bot; anything about a firing goes to Kiln Bot.
