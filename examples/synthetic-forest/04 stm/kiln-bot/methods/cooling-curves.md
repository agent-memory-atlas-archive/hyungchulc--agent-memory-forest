# Cooling curves

How Kiln Bot watches a firing climb and cool, and what it learned doing it.

- It reads the controller's temperature every minute and compares the climb with the last three runs of
  the same program.
- New elements make a firing climb faster: on 2042-05-13 the climb was ahead of the last three runs from
  the third hour and the firing reached cone 6 forty minutes early. Watch the last hour of the first
  firings after a repair closely and tell Studio Hub when the climb runs ahead.
