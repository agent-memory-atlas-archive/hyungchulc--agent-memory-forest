# Kiln Two: what it is

Kiln Two is the larger of the studio's two electric kilns: a 7 cubic-foot top loader, three-phase, with
four elements and a programmable controller that Kiln Bot reads and sets. Juno calls it "K2" or "the big
kiln". It stands in the back room of the studio on Quay Row, vented through the north wall.

## Timeline

- 2039-08-02 America/Halifax — Kiln Two delivered to the studio's first room and wired in.
- 2041-09-15 America/Halifax — moved with the studio to the old net loft on Quay Row and vented through the north wall.
