# Kiln Two repairs

Repairs of Kiln Two, newest first, one section per repair.

## 2042-05-06 Element fault

The kiln stopped at 940 °C during a bisque firing and the controller showed error E-1 (the temperature
not rising). Kiln Bot reported it to Studio Hub, which told Juno. Juno found two of the four elements
broken.

- 2042-05-08 — two replacement elements ordered: recorded in `04 stm/admin/shops/cinderwell-supply.md`.

Juno fitted the two new elements herself and a test firing to 600 °C ran clean.
