# studio

## Scope

Brindlemoor Clayworks, Juno's pottery studio on Quay Row in Saltmere: the equipment it runs, what it makes
and fires, its suppliers' deliveries for the studio, its commissions and customers.

## Patterns and rules

- Glaze loads are fired by Kiln Bot on the program Juno picks; Juno unloads and checks every load herself (as of 2042-05-13; source `04 stm/studio/kiln-two/firings-2042-q2.md`)

## Current state

- Kiln Two is back in service after the May element fault; glaze loads fire to cone 5 for now (as of 2042-05-14; source `04 stm/studio/kiln-two/settings.md`)

## Branches

- `kiln-two` — the studio's large electric kiln, bought in 2039; holds what it is, its firings, its repairs and its firing limits.
